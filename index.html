FEATURED PROJECT

SignCare — Virtual Gesture Communication for the Hearing‑Impaired

PythonTensorFlow/KerasOpenCV MediaPipeStreamlitNLTK

An end-to-end Indian Sign Language platform enabling real-time gesture-to-text recognition and speech-to-ISL translation, supporting Telugu and English. A Keras neural network classifies gestures from MediaPipe hand-landmark features (126-dim, two-hand pose) through a Streamlit + WebRTC pipeline, while a speech-to-ISL pipeline uses Google Speech Recognition and NLTK preprocessing to sequence and auto-concatenate matching ISL videos — with frame-skipping and prediction smoothing for fast, offline-capable inference.
